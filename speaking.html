Speaking

I enjoy speaking mostly for the questions afterwards. They are always the best part, and I usually learn more from the audience than they learn from me.

Things I can talk about#

  • AI in the OKR cycle.

    What to automate and what to keep human. AI can draft, check and summarise. Deciding what is worth achieving is still a human conversation.

  • AI in the classroom, honestly.

    What has worked at national scale in Malaysia, what has not, and why the hard part is never the model. Grounding AI in the syllabus, keeping it correct in English and Bahasa Melayu, and keeping it cheap enough for every school.

  • AI for the last billion.

    Building for students on the cheapest phone, with patchy internet, in their first language. Why rural users should shape the architecture, not just the marketing.

  • Working with government.

    Lessons from building BORANG and from working with the Ministry of Education and state agencies. Patience, trust, and writing the need statement clearly.

  • Agentic AI for public sector teams.

    Practical sessions on n8n, Claude Code and the Model Context Protocol for people who are not engineers.

Formats I enjoy#

Fireside chats, panels with people who disagree with me, workshops with teachers, and small rooms where everyone gets to ask a question. I am also happy to speak in Bahasa Melayu.
