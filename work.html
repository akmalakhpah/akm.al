What I work on

My work sits in three areas, and this is the order they come in: OKRs, education and government. They look different from the outside, but they share one idea: technology should make life a little easier for people who are already working hard. Team leaders. Students. Teachers. Civil servants.

Everything below was built by teams. Where I say “I”, it is only to explain my part.

OKR#

OKR Institute

Co-founder & CTO · Kuala Lumpur, serving clients worldwide

okrinstitute.org

OKR Institute helps organisations set and run OKRs well, through certification, coaching and community. It works with teams from small companies to Fortune 500 firms across Asia, Europe, the Middle East and the Americas.

What I do: I lead technology strategy and the platforms behind OKRs, KPIs, check-ins and 360 reviews.

What the team has built:

  • An AI OKR assistant that helps leaders draft and critique objectives, spot misalignment across teams, and turn weekly check-in notes into something a leader can act on.

  • Connectors for Claude and ChatGPT, so people can work with their goals inside the AI assistant they already use, instead of logging into yet another tool.

  • World OKR Summit, an online gathering of practitioners from more than 40 countries. I have shared how we run AI-native OKRs at Pandai there, and I walked away having learned more than I gave.

FlowyTeam

Co-founder & CTO · since January 2020 · Kuala Lumpur

flowyteam.com

FlowyTeam helps companies build high-performance teams. People plan, manage and link their goals through OKRs, KPIs, check-ins and 360 reviews, in one app, with humans and AI agents working side by side.

OpenOKR

Open source · AGPL-3.0

github.com/open-okr/open-okr

An open-source platform where teams set their goals, plan the work that moves them, and track both in one place. It covers objectives, key results, KPIs, weekly check-ins and quarterly reviews, and teams can host it themselves.

EdTech#

Pandai

Co-founder & CTO · since December 2019 · Kuala Lumpur

pandai.org

Pandai is a learning app for Malaysian school students. It started as gamified practice questions aligned to the national syllabus. Today it includes live tuition, exam practice, progress reports for parents, a teacher app, and a set of AI tools.

What I do: I lead technology, data and AI. That covers engineering, product, content systems and infrastructure.

What the team has built:

  • PBot, an AI study buddy that answers students in their own language and stays inside the syllabus.

  • AI Grader, which helps teachers mark open response work. It gives each teacher back around 20 hours a week, time that goes back into teaching.

  • AI Question Builder, which turns curriculum material into practice and assessment items teachers can actually use.

  • Pandai Teacher, used by around 100,000 teachers, most of whom use its AI assistant.

Where it is today:

  • More than2 millionregistered learners across Malaysia, Brunei and Singapore
  • More than150,000students learning every day
  • More than500 millionlearning activities completed since 2020
  • Reaching10,000 schoolsand around 100,000 teachers
  • Roughly1 in 4of our users live in rural areas
  • These numbers belong to students, parents, teachers and a team that works very hard. I just get to share them.
  • A partner for the Ministry of Education’s AI Powered Classroom pilot
  • Working with the Sarawak Economic Development Foundation to reach schools in Borneo, starting with SMK Demak Baru

Among students who use PBot, 65% see their final exam results improve. That number, and the hours we give back to teachers, are the reasons I think the AI work is worth doing at all.

P

P&AI

Practice & AI · Open source

github.com/p-n-ai

An open-source AI learning system, built on what we learned scaling Pandai. Instead of waiting for students to ask, it starts study sessions with them through chat.

  • PAI Bot, a self-hostable AI learning agent that teaches through chat, tracks mastery and schedules reviews.

  • Open School Syllabus (OSS), a structured, machine-readable repository of school curricula for AI agents and education tools.

F

FDE Training

12-week hybrid programme

training-ai-fde

A programme that takes fresh computer science and engineering graduates and turns them into deployable AI Forward Deployed Engineers. They can already write code, but have not yet shipped software that real users depend on. No AI or machine learning background is needed.

GovTech#

Aidan Technologies

Founder & Director · since 2007 · Kuala Lumpur

aidan.com.my aidantech.com

Aidan started as a small digital agency with a big hope: to make dealing with government simpler for ordinary people.

What the team has built:

  • AI assistants inside government agencies, designed with the officers who use them.

  • Training on AI and agentic AI for public sector teams, using tools such as n8n and Claude Code.

BORANG

Launched in 2019

borang.my

A Government-as-a-Platform that lets agencies build and run digital services for the public without starting from scratch every time. Teams build forms, send submissions to the right approvers, and track them on a live dashboard.
