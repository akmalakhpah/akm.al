Hi, I’m Akmal.

I am an engineer and an entrepreneur from Malaysia. I like solving real problems. I run businesses and build high-performing teams so I can do just that, and I still enjoy writing the software that helps us solve them.

Most of my work sits in three areas: OKRs, education and government. I started in big organisations, at Intel and then the World Health Organization, and I have been co-founding technology companies since 2007, alongside people who teach me something new every week.

What I spend my days on

Goals that teams can actually use.

At OKR Institute we help organisations set honest goals and check in on them without drowning in spreadsheets. FlowyTeam and OpenOKR give teams the software to run them on.

OKR

AI that helps people learn.

Pandai helps Malaysian students practise and do better in school, with tools like PBot and AI Grader. P&AI shares this work as open source, and our FDE Training prepares fresh graduates to deploy AI.

EdTech

Technology for the public sector.

At Aidan we help government agencies use AI assistants in their daily work, and we train public sector teams to build with tools like n8n and Claude Code. BORANG lets agencies build digital services without starting from scratch.
